Guidelines on How to Play Volleyball



📖 RULES & REGULATIONS


  • Serve behind the Yellow Line
  • Rotate clockwise
  • Do not touch the net with your hands
  • Do not cross the center line with any part of your body

🏐 EQUIPMENT NEEDED


  • MIKASA Volleyball
  • Volleyball Net
  • Jersey with Number
  • Knee Pads & Elbow Pads

🏆ROLES OF COURT PLAYERS


  • Outside Hitter & Opposite Hitter
  • Middle Blocker
  • Setter
  • Libero
